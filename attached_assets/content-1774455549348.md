## The Crisis

Rural Mali faces extreme challenges without reliable access to clean water.

![Education Interrupted](https://sunpoweredwaterworks.org/images/children-school.png)

### Education Interrupted

Girls frequently miss school to fetch water, walking miles each day. Without water, their education—and future—is put on hold.

![Health at Risk](https://sunpoweredwaterworks.org/images/hero-mali.png)

### Health at Risk

1 in 3 health centers in rural Mali lack basic water services. Families face constant threats from waterborne diseases like cholera and dysentery.

![Stunted Agriculture](https://sunpoweredwaterworks.org/images/community.png)

### Stunted Agriculture

Poor irrigation severely limits farming in a region that is 65% desert or semi-desert. Without water, crops fail and communities hunger.

![A Daily Burden](https://sunpoweredwaterworks.org/images/women-water.png)

### A Daily Burden

Women and girls bear the brunt of the crisis, often spending hours every single day just trying to secure water for their families.

## Sun-Powered. Community-Built.  Life-Changing.

A sustainable, gravity-fed system powered entirely by the abundant Malian sun.

### 1.5kW Solar Array

6 × 250W panels harness abundant solar energy.

### Solar Water Pump

Efficiently extracts deep groundwater using solar power.

### 10,000L Tank

High-capacity storage ensures continuous supply.

### 10M Tower

Elevates the tank to create natural water pressure.

### Gravity System

Requires no secondary pumps to distribute water.

### Distribution Network

Pipes deliver clean water directly to village centers.

![Solar Water System](https://sunpoweredwaterworks.org/images/water-system.png)

A completely self-sustaining model delivering 10,000+ liters daily.
